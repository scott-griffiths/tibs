But why is it called tibs?
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Because 'tibs' is (almost) 'bits' backwards. It's also distinctive, and the name was available on PyPI.

It's got nothing to do with Ethiopian stew. Or cats.
